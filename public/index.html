Calculation & cache details

Units × price, rounded to cents per fund, then summed. Unavailable funds are excluded from partial totals. History holds current units constant and uses the same included funds throughout. Its last date can differ from the headline, which uses each fund's latest price.

Prices and history are cached for 1 hour; symbols for 7 days. Refresh retries prices after 60 seconds. Unresolved mappings are cached for 6 hours. Rate limits may extend the wait.
