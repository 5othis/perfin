How this chart is calculated

Current units are held constant and multiplied by historical end-of-day prices / NAVs. Combined values use only shared price dates for the same included funds throughout. Missing prices are not estimated, zero-filled or carried forward. Lines connect available observations, not inferred daily prices. The final date can differ from the headline. No purchase dates, past units, cash flows or reinvestments are inferred; raw prices are not dividend/split-adjusted.

Calculation & cache details

Units × price, rounded to cents per fund, then summed. Unavailable funds are excluded from partial totals. History holds current units constant and uses the same included funds throughout. Its last date can differ from the headline, which uses each fund's latest price.

Prices and history are cached for 1 hour; symbols for 7 days. Refresh retries prices after 60 seconds. Unresolved mappings are cached for 6 hours. Rate limits may extend the wait.
